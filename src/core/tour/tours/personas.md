# Personas

## center
A persona is who you're in a chat: a name, an avatar and a description the model reads.

## .personaList
Every persona you've made. Click one to edit it.

## .personaAvatarRow
The name and avatar. The name replaces {{user}} in prompts.

## .personaBlurb
What the model is told about you. Kept short, it's sent with every message.

## .colorsGroup
The colour used for your messages in a chat.
